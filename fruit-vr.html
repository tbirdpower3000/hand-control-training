🍉 Fruit Slicer VR

For a phone in a Google Cardboard viewer. Fruit is thrown up in front of you; cut it before it falls.

Blade

How to play

1. Press Start and allow motion (and the camera, if asked). 2. Turn the phone sideways and put it in the viewer. 3. A round lasts 60 seconds. When it ends, look at the panel for two seconds, or press the viewer's button, to play again. Turn the phone upright to come back here.

Open on your phone

Scan this with the phone's camera, or with the scanner in the browser you want to play in. It opens this page.

Your rounds

Rounds are kept in this browser only. The camera picture is used on this phone to find your hand and is never stored or sent anywhere. Each round records how often swipes cut, how fast they were, how often the hand was lost, and how far off-centre the blade passed, so the aim can be corrected for the next round.
