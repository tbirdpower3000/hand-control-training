# Privacy and disclaimers

**The camera stays on your computer.** The training page asks for your webcam so a hand-tracking library (Google MediaPipe, running in your browser) can find your hand. The video is processed on your device and is never sent anywhere, recorded or saved. Nothing from the camera leaves your computer.

**What is saved.** A profile of a few numbers (how far your hand comfortably reaches, your pinch threshold, how much the cursor wobbles, how strongly buttons should pull the cursor in, your answers and ratings) is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays in that browser unless you download it or share it. "Reset to defaults" deletes it.

**Sharing is optional and public.** If you press "Share results with the project", the page opens a GitHub issue pre-filled with those numbers, your browser type and screen size. Nothing is sent until you press Submit on GitHub. The issue is public, is posted under your GitHub username, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies. You need a GitHub account, which GitHub offers to people 13 and older. Students at a school should check with a teacher or parent before sharing. A bot stores the numbers in this repository, where anyone can read them, and closes the issue. To have a shared result removed, open an issue saying which result number.

**Third parties your browser contacts.** Loading the page fetches the hand-tracking library from jsDelivr and its model from Google's storage, and fonts from Google Fonts; those services see your IP address like any website you visit. The page itself has no analytics, cookies or accounts.

**Not a medical test.** This page adjusts a game-like control. It does not measure, assess or diagnose anything about your health, coordination, tremor or motor skills, and its numbers should not be used for that.

**No warranty.** The page and the atlas are educational tools provided as they are, without any warranty.
